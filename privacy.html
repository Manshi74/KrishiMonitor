PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

KrishiMonitor stores your name, normalized email address, password hash, email verification state, and account timestamps. Passwords are never stored as plain text. Login sessions and expiring verification/reset token hashes are stored by the server.

Your farm records

When signed in, saved crop records and analysis reports are associated with your account. Crop photos and AI chat messages are not stored by this application. If you choose AI analysis, the photo or question is sent to Google Gemini; review Google's data terms before sharing sensitive information.

Email and security

The configured SMTP provider delivers verification and password reset links. Links expire and are single use. Do not forward them. The site uses an HttpOnly session cookie and CSRF protection for private record changes.

Retention and requests

Account and saved record data remain in MongoDB until deleted by the site operator or removed through account deletion support. To request access or deletion, contact hello@rojtech.com. No payment processing is connected.

This notice describes current application behavior; the operator should confirm it against the final hosting, retention, and legal requirements before public launch.
